How I would use these prices

Buy the K2 at $349.00 if you want a smaller enclosed Creality and the official $200.00 markdown from $549.00 still holds in the cart. Pay $449.00 for the Combo only if you actually want that bundle; check the live page for what ships with it. This is not the same machine as the larger K2 Plus in my SV08 vs K2 Plus guide.

Buy the SV08 at $529.00 when you want official 350 mm volume at the lower Sovol price. Step up to the SV08 MAX at $1,049.00 only if you need that larger machine; confirm the US-warehouse variant at checkout. The Sovol links are for the official US store, and that program is US-only.

If wet filament is the problem, start with the official dryer prices above and see the SH01 vs SH02 vs SH03 guide for the official temperature and spool-count differences.

On Anycubic, the Kobra 4 at $279.00 is the lower official FDM price I verified today. The Kobra X (4 Colors) at $299.00 is the 4-color variant on the official Kobra X page. The Photon Mono M7 at $429.00 is a resin printer, not an FDM substitute. If a Photon print is failing to stick, use the Photon adhesion guide and official Standard Resin at $21.99 rather than guessing exposure times.
