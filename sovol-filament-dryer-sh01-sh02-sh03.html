Which dryer should you buy?

Start with the official temperature range you actually need, then the official spool count, then the price. The SH01 is the one I would buy first for PLA-range drying at $37.00, as long as 50°C and two spools are enough. That official 40–50°C window matches the kind of PLA drying many manufacturers put near 50°C; I am not turning that into a guarantee for every spool or a retraction setting. If PLA is already stringing, dry the spool first, then follow the order in Why is my PLA stringing?

Pay $18.00 more for the SH02 when you want official 70°C headroom and the nine material presets. That is the cleaner official path for PETG, ABS, ASA, nylon, and similar types listed on that page. The official heat-up claims (50°C in 7 minutes, 70°C in 25 minutes) are manufacturer figures, not timings I recorded.

Pay $109.00 for the SH03 when four spools or two independent chambers are the point. Official working temperature goes to 85°C, and the page lists an auto dehumidification system. I am not treating that as a reason to skip checking the live humidity reading on the machine you receive.

These are official US-store prices. If you are buying outside the US, do not assume the same offer. Confirm plug type and the ship-from country in the cart.

FAQ

Is the SH01 enough if I only print PLA?

For official 40–50°C drying of two 1 kg spools, yes, that is the job the SH01 page describes. If you later need 70°C or 85°C materials, the SH02 or SH03 official ranges are the reason to step up.

Does the SH01 include desiccant?

The official SH01 page says you can put four bags of desiccant inside and that they are not included in the package.
